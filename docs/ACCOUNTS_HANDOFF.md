History uses latest-per-date correction handling with carry-in seeds. A history point must state which accounts have balances and carry-forward ages, rather than silently treating missing values as zero.

Current net worth excludes archived and include_in_net_worth=false accounts. It sums checking/savings/investment/asset/legacy-other as Assets and subtracts signed credit/loan/mortgage amounts owed as Debts. Negative liabilities are credits; negative bank balances reduce assets. Checking and savings are cash, not investments/property. Never combine currencies without a deliberate verified FX feature. Show partial/missing and stale coverage prominently. The current section subtotals intentionally show all active listed balances, including excluded accounts; these are not net worth totals.

Types and currency are immutable after account creation to protect history and card-payment semantics. Source account metadata may mark currency as assumed; do not silently relabel or convert balances. Property/other values are estimates, investments use Market Value, bank accounts Balance, debts Amount owed. Existing source imports retain their earlier contract and do not populate balance snapshots. Archive is a recoverable visibility/net-worth setting, not deletion of historical ledger data or a ban on old imports.
